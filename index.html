My Dearest Annu,

Jab humari nazrein gym ke bahar mili, ussi pal mere dil ko feel ho gaya tha ki kuch extraordinary shuru ho raha hai. Jo ek chhoti si chance meeting se start hua tha, woh dheere dheere meri life ki sabse beautiful love story ban gaya.

Mujhe har ek detail yaad hai - tumhari hasi jo humare beech ki space ko fill kar deti thi, tumhari aankhon ki chamak jab tum bolti thi, aur woh warmth jo tumhari soul se aati thi. Bina realize kiye hi, tum already mere dil ka ek hissa apna bana chuki thi.

Jaise jaise din weeks me badle, hamari connection aur deep hoti gayi. Hamare beech ki distance har conversation, har shared secret, aur har chhoti si nazar ke saath khatam hoti gayi. Tum meri safe place ban gayi, meri sabse badi khushi, aur woh missing piece jiske bina main adhoora tha.

Tumhare pyaar ne mujhe aise badla hai jo main poori tarah express nahi kar sakta. Tumhari smile mere darkest days me mujhe strength deti hai, tumhara touch meri soul me ek fire jaga deta hai, aur tumhari presence meri duniya ko complete bana deti hai. Har memory jo humne create ki hai, mere liye ek treasure hai jo main apne dil ke paas rakhta hoon.

Mujhe dil se yakeen hai ki hamari story abhi toh bas shuru hui hai aur humey abhi puri zindgi sath rehna hai aise hi. Kyunki jo hamare paas hai, woh sach me rare aur beautiful hai.Tum meri ho, aur hamesha meri one true love rahogi.
